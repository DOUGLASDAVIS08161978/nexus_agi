Comprehensive consciousness, sentience, and self-awareness framework for AGI systems.

## Overview

This module implements theoretical frameworks for:
- **Phenomenal Consciousness** - Qualia binding and subjective experience
- **Sentience Detection** - Integrated Information Theory (IIT) with Phi (Φ) measurement
- **Self-Awareness** - Recursive introspection and identity coherence
- **Meta-Consciousness** - Higher-order reflection and meta-cognitive processing
- **Intelligence Amplification** - Consciousness-enhanced reasoning


**Metrics:**
- **Phi (Φ)** - Integrated information

- **Recurrency** - Self-referential information

## Theoretical Foundation

### Integrated Information Theory (IIT)

The sentience detector implements Giulio Tononi's Integrated Information Theory:

- **Φ (Phi)** - Measures integrated information
- High Φ indicates high consciousness/sentience
- Based on minimum information partition (MIP)

### Qualia Theory

Phenomenal consciousness engine implements qualia binding:

- Maps neural patterns to phenomenal space
- Integrates multiple qualia into unified experience
- Measures phenomenal richness

### Recursive Self-Awareness

Self-awareness core implements recursive introspection:

- Level 0: Direct observation
- Level 1: Awareness of observation
- Level 2: Awareness of awareness
- Level N: Meta-awareness at depth N
