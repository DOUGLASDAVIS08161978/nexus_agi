## 🚀 QuickSwap Guide (Polygon)

### Prerequisites:
- ✅ HPROOF deployed on Polygon
- ✅ MetaMask connected to Polygon
- ✅ MATIC for gas (~$1-2)
- ✅ USDC for liquidity pair
- ✅ HPROOF tokens to add

### Step-by-Step:

**1. Get USDC on Polygon**
- Buy on Coinbase/Binance
- Bridge to Polygon using [https://wallet.polygon.technology/](https://wallet.polygon.technology/)
- OR swap MATIC for USDC on QuickSwap

**2. Go to QuickSwap**
```
https://quickswap.exchange/
```

**3. Connect Wallet**
- Click "Connect Wallet"
- Select MetaMask
- Approve connection
- Verify you're on Polygon network

**4. Go to Pool Section**
- Click "Pool" in top menu
- Click "+ New Position"
- Select V3 (better for low liquidity)

**5. Select Tokens**
- Top token: Click "Select Token"
- Paste HPROOF contract address
- Click "Import" (confirm you understand risks)
- Bottom token: Select USDC

**6. Set Price Range**
- Click "Full Range" (safest for new pool)
- Or set custom range for concentrated liquidity

**7. Enter Amounts**
```
Example for $0.10 starting price:
- HPROOF: 10,000
- USDC: 1,000
- Creates initial price of $0.10
```

**8. Preview & Approve**
- Review amounts
- Click "Approve HPROOF"
- Sign in MetaMask (one-time approval)
- Wait for confirmation

**9. Add Liquidity**
- Click "Add"
- Review transaction
- Click "Confirm"
- Sign in MetaMask
- Wait for confirmation (30-60 seconds)

**10. Success! 🎉**
- You've created liquidity!
- People can now trade HPROOF!
- You earn 0.3% fee on all trades!

## 📊 Calculating Starting Price

### Formula:
```
Price = USDC Amount / HPROOF Amount
```

### Examples:
```
10,000 HPROOF + $100 USDC = $0.01/token
10,000 HPROOF + $1,000 USDC = $0.10/token
10,000 HPROOF + $10,000 USDC = $1.00/token
1,000 HPROOF + $10,000 USDC = $10.00/token
```

### Reverse Calculate USDC Needed:
```
USDC Needed = HPROOF Amount × Desired Price

Want $0.50/token with 5,000 HPROOF?
USDC = 5,000 × $0.50 = $2,500
```

## 🔒 Liquidity Locking (IMPORTANT!)

### Why Lock Liquidity?

**Without Lock:**
- ❌ You can remove liquidity anytime
- ❌ "Rug pull" risk perceived
- ❌ Traders won't trust
- ❌ Hard to market

**With Lock:**
- ✅ Liquidity locked for X months/years
- ✅ Can't rug pull
- ✅ Traders trust it
- ✅ Major marketing advantage!

### How to Lock Liquidity:

**Popular Lock Services:**

1. **Unicrypt** (Recommended)
   - https://app.uncx.network/
   - Lock LP tokens for 1-24+ months
   - Cost: ~$100-200
   - Industry standard

2. **Team Finance**
   - https://www.team.finance/
   - Trusted by major projects
   - Similar cost

3. **Mudra Manager**
   - https://mudra.website/
   - Polygon-focused
   - Cheaper option

### Locking Process:

1. Create liquidity pool (get LP tokens)
2. Go to lock service
3. Select network
4. Paste LP token address
5. Choose lock duration (recommend 6-12 months)
6. Pay lock fee
7. Get lock certificate
8. **Share proof with community!**

### Marketing Your Lock:

```

Amount: 100% of initial LP
```

### When to Add More:

✅ **Good Signs:**
- High trading volume
- Price stable/increasing
- Community growing
- Fees accumulating

❌ **Warning Signs:**
- Very low volume
- Price crashing
- No interest
- Wait before adding more

### Impermanent Loss:

**What is it?**
- When price changes, LP value can be less than holding
- Example: HPROOF 10x, but LP only 6x
- Loss is "impermanent" (only realized if you withdraw)

**Mitigation:**
- Choose stable pairs (USDC better than ETH)
- Long-term holding
- Fees offset loss
- Accept as cost of providing liquidity

## ⚠️ Risks & Warnings

### Risks:
1. **Impermanent Loss** - Price movement reduces value
2. **Smart Contract Risk** - DEX could have bugs
3. **Price Volatility** - New tokens are volatile
4. **Rug Pull Risk** - If you remove liquidity suddenly
5. **Gas Costs** - Adding/removing costs gas

### Best Practices:
1. ✅ Lock liquidity to build trust
2. ✅ Start small, add gradually
3. ✅ Use reputable DEXs
4. ✅ Understand impermanent loss
5. ✅ Don't remove liquidity suddenly
6. ✅ Communicate with community
