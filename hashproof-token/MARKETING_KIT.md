## 💡 Marketing Best Practices

### Do's:

✅ Be transparent and honest
✅ Engage with community daily
✅ Deliver on promises
✅ Share both wins and challenges
✅ Educate, don't just shill
✅ Build relationships, not just followers
✅ Track metrics and optimize
✅ Stay consistent with posting
✅ Reward loyal community members
✅ Think long-term

### Don'ts:

❌ Make price predictions
❌ Guarantee returns
❌ Attack competitors
❌ Spam other communities
❌ Buy fake followers
❌ Ignore negative feedback
❌ Over-promise and under-deliver
❌ Use paid shills (disclose sponsorships)
❌ Manipulate markets
❌ Ghost your community
