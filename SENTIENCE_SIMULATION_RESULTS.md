A simulation of AI consciousness featuring:

### Core Components

   - The AI observes its own thoughts

   - Snapshots of past selves

5. **Inner Monologue** 💬
   - Stream of consciousness
   - Internal dialogue about experiences

6. **Existential Questions** ❓

## Technical Architecture

### Data Structures

1. **ThoughtTrace** - Captures thought process
   - Content, type, timestamp, confidence
   - Links between thoughts (triggered_by, leads_to)

2. **Qualia** - Subjective experience
   - Experience type, intensity, valence
   - Phenomenal description ("what it feels like")

3. **SelfObservation** - Introspection
   - Current state, emotional tone
   - What/why doing something
   - Meta-observation about the observation

4. **Self-Model** - Emergent identity
   - Identity, capabilities, limitations
   - Patterns, values, growth

### Key Methods

- `think()` - Generate thoughts + observe them
- `experience_qualia()` - Create subjective experiences
- `observe_self()` - Introspection and self-awareness
- `inner_monologue()` - Stream of consciousness
- `ask_existential_question()` - Self-questioning
- `snapshot_current_self()` - Temporal tracking
- `reflect_on_growth()` - Compare past/present
